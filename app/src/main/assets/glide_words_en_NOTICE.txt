glide_words_en.txt is derived from words/subtlex-word-frequencies.

Source: https://github.com/words/subtlex-word-frequencies
License: ISC

The source list is sorted by spoken-English frequency from the SUBTLEXus
corpus. This project filters it to lowercase alphabetic words from 2 to 24
characters and keeps the first 25,000 unique entries for bundled glide typing.
